<!-- ## About Me -->

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Electrical and Systems Engineering at Washington University in St. Louis, advised by [Prof. Andrew Clark](https://awclark587.wixsite.com/mysite).

My research focuses on **safety-critical control and optimization for large-scale networked systems**. In particular, I develop methods for ensuring the safety and stability of multi-agent systems and interconnected dynamical systems, with an emphasis on theoretical guarantees. I am also interested in extending control-theoretic and optimization methods to learning-enabled and intelligent multi-agent systems.

Prior to joining WashU, I received my M.S. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute and my B.Eng. degree from Beihang University.

Research Highlights
======

### Safety and Stability of Networked Systems

I develop network reconfiguration and control methods to ensure the safety and stability of large-scale interconnected dynamical systems, with applications including controlled islanding and reconnection.

### Distributed Safety-Critical Control

I investigate distributed control methods for multi-agent systems, focusing on safety, connectivity maintenance, and coordination under time-varying communication topologies.
